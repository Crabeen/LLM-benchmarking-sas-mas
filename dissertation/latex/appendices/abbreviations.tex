\correct{} & Label value \emph{correct}, for either the Discovery or the Endorsement axis \\
\wrong{} & Label value \emph{wrong} \\
\uncertain{} & Label value \emph{uncertain}, reserved for genuinely unresolvable cases such as truncation inside the answer-bearing statement \\
\ctow{} & Harmful transition: the source state endorses the reference answer and the final state endorses a wrong answer \\
\wtoc{} & Beneficial transition: the source state is wrong and the final state endorses the reference answer \\
$D(s)$ & Discovery label of stage $s$: whether the stage contains enough content to support the reference answer \\
$E(s)$ & Endorsement label of stage $s$: whether the stage commits to that answer \\
$T_i$ & The ordered sequence of observable stage texts produced for item $i$, ending in the final output $f_i$ \\
$n(x)$ & Normalisation function applied before lexical comparison: lowercasing, punctuation and article removal, whitespace collapse \\
$G$ & Accepted reference set for an item: the gold answer together with its supplied aliases \\
$\kappa$ & Cohen's kappa, chance-corrected agreement \citep{cohen1960coefficient} \\
$W_h$ & Finite-population weight of frozen sampling stratum $h$ (\Cref{eq:weighting}) \\
\texttt{v3b} & The project's merged supervised fine-tuning checkpoint of Qwen3-4B \\
